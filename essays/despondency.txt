# Despondency

As the aging clock tolls by
a desolate shadow softly sighs
and wishes their dreams goodbye

It peers off into the mourning sky
stained blankets of water appear to fly
as the world sings off its lullaby

Numerous threads pierce through the weary eyes
stringing wisps of emotions to things that might belie
the melancholic promises that were tied with a cry

An outstretched hand is aimed at the white evanescing dye
grasping, no, missing the enchanting illusions of a blue butterfly
as the future interlaced with the colorless past, whispers to the present goodbye.
