# A Mountain Backdrop

-Poof

A leather football rolls into a grassy field surrounded by an expansive mountain backdrop. 
As the ball zings through the cool air again, and again, before occasionally bouncing off 
the felted floor, soft chirps dance in the background and antlers stride into view. Could 
such a sport be played in a place not as idyllic? For sure, but I feel there is something 
special about a common pastime among those who have traveled to such a faraway place. Mountains 
were scaled and nature was seen in all of its serenity, but doesn’t everyone do that? There’s 
beauty in the collective appreciation of God’s creation, but there is just something so specific, 
so personal about the zips through the sky. It was our last breath of open air before the fluorescent 
lights and glowing inboxes of our corporate lives, and the ball was the one thing we carried up 
the mountain that had no use except to be thrown.

While the ball flies in a crevice of the mountain range, to me it seems rather that the ball 
flies at the summit, the summit of all the years of pain and studying, of laughter and tears, 
of reflections and mistakes, of our briefly intermingled lives, built up yet gone in an instant. 
The mountain of our college experience is perhaps  so ordinary compared the special place we're in, 
but that is what gives this whole trip its meaning before it’s all over.

-zip

And the ball fell back down, dropped from worn, red hands.
