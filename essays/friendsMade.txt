# The Friends We Made Along the way

After being almost 70% done with college (⅝ semesters finished), the feeling of saying goodbye to good friends is not something that is foreign to me. Whether it's to study abroad, graduate, transfer schools, or drop out to do YC, people often leave our lives. While we could look back and reminisce about the past, I think these experiences should instead motivate us to enjoy the present and the rare time in our lives when our paths intersect at Notre Dame ~~
