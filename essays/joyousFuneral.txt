# Our Joyous Funeral Procession

Picture melting candlelight. Picture flowers that are beautiful and a little bit dying. Picture toasts to the ones far away and toasts to the ones we never lost. Picture dancing so hard you forget to be remorseful about anything. Picture someone crying, happy crying, the kind that happens when you realize you made it through a school year of ups and downs, of regrets and satisfaction, of wishes to skip forward or freeze in time, but regardless of what you do now, it's sealed away in a framed memory. 

This is a night for nostalgia that doesn’t sting. For missing people you love and knowing they’ll be back. For saying farewell to those by your side but are now off to greener pastures. For the year that passed and change that is destined to come.

We won’t be back here again, so let us write our own happy ending ~~
