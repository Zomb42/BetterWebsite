# Fluttering Perceptions

Like how autumn leaves flutter down in colorful piles, so too, I believe, are our perceptions of others.
They start off green, somewhat unnoticeable to our eyes during the summer haze, before a vivid transformation, 
as gusts of memories sway by, melting our chaotic essence into their pelts and all that is within. Then they 
shine, but seemingly only for a moment, in warm contours, before flicking away and dancing in life's rays. Still 
bright, still precious, but never growing anymore. They stay though, in plentiful heaps, to be revisited and 
jumped into perhaps from time to time, but their color doesn't grow brighter and only crystallizes as time 
passes. Perhaps one day they rot or simply disintegrate into time, but I don't think that ever changes what 
they once were ~
