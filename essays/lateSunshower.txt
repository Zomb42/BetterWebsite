# A Late Sunshower
Format: poetry

(The 4th stanza is lost to history, but I feel like it just makes it more bittersweet)


The skylight dazzles all around
shattering into fragments 
of glossy glass
encapsulating all 
one might see 
with a moist, evaporating, kiss

A wilted rose takes in the rainbow
fragrant lights cascade upon petals
and intercede
the fettered past 
to the naked eye
through its elegant, elusive, glow

Breaths of mist shroud the leaves
Licking the air with sweet pearls 
it gently seduces 
with evaporating dew
veiling a crackled husk
by an evanescing, delicate, shadow

A weathered women plucks the flower
admiring the lucid curves
that sway
and bend
in the daunting wind
before disintegrating in her frail hands ~

## Haiku Version

Glistening rainbows,
captivated in droplets,
fall in sunny mists
