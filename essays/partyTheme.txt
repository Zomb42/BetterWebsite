## The Friends We Made Along the way 

1/18/2026

After being almost 70% done with college (⅝ semesters finished), the feeling of saying goodbye to good friends 
is not something that is foreign to me. Whether it's to study abroad, graduate, transfer schools, or drop out to do YC, 
people often leave our lives. While we could look back and reminisce about the past, I think these experiences should 
instead motivate us to enjoy the present and the rare time in our lives when our paths intersect at Notre Dame ~~




## Our Joyous Funeral Procession

4/23/2026

Picture melting candlelight. Picture flowers that are beautiful and a little bit dying. Picture toasts to the ones far away and 
toasts to the ones we never lost. Picture dancing so hard you forget to be remorseful about anything. Picture someone crying, 
happy crying, the kind that happens when you realize you made it through a school year of ups and downs, of regrets and 
satisfaction, of wishes to skip forward or freeze in time, but regardless of what you do now, it's sealed away in a framed memory. 

This is a night for nostalgia that doesn’t sting. For missing people you love and knowing they’ll be back. For saying farewell 
to those by your side but are now off to greener pastures. For the year that passed and change that is destined to come.

We won’t be back here again, so let us write our own happy ending ~~
