# Wispy Winter Wonderland

It was a cold yet dreamy winter night. As we stood in the overhang by the pink glow of touchdown Jesus and outskirts of the fairy lighted library lawn, the snow danced across the sky, twirling in the soft glow of the spotlights directed above. Everything seemed to have shined for a second, if only for just a second, as the frost blanketed the chairs, the tables, and the trees all in front of us and glistened across the vast lawn to our right. Quiet murmurs lived in the background of passer by students as the wind whipped across our faces. Making noses and ears glow a soft red in this calm snowy, illuminated night. A bit cold, a bit painful, yet ultimately warm from within. It was almost perfect in some crystalized sense.
