# Let me bade you goodbye

Let me breathe in your daydream one last time
Let me cry in your embrace one last night
Of all those times where you lead me to endeavors
I will burn you into my eyelids forever
I still love you, even though I will miss you now

Let me soak in our bittersweet memories once more
just one more caress to remember your warmth
just one last kiss to seal my precious love away
before it all comes to an idyllic end today
So, before my hope dies, let me bade you goodbye
